Bug fixes:
- Header on side screens (playlist detail, song detail, album detail,...) had an unneeded padding
- If a light mode was selected, but the device had dark mode set, the status bar icons remained white

Additions:
- Songs in user-created playlists and in favourites can now be sorted without breaking the original order
- Favourites can now be reordered
- Albums' artists are now derived from the songs that they contain
- Songs can be filtered, so that they are not seen in the app (but can be put back from settings)
- Automatic playlists now have translated titles
