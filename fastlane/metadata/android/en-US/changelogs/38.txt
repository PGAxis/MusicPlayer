Bug fixes:
- Cover art in the notification and on the lock screen now loads faster, since the cover art cache was not being used
- Songs, albums and artists are now sorted case-insensitively and by your device language (e.g. in Czech, Č now comes after C, not after Z like before). Accented letters are listed with their base letter (Á with A)

Additions:
- Importing a playlist now pre-fills and selects its name from the file name
- The cover art on the song detail screen is now much bigger
- New setting to limit how many songs in the queue keep their album art loaded (explanation in settings)
- Reordering the queue is smoother: the move is applied when you drop the song (or immediately if the currently playing song is moved, or if the song being moved gets immediately before/after the currently playing song)
- The settings file is now defragmented automatically when it gets bloated (mine shrank about 20x after almost a year without defragmentation)
